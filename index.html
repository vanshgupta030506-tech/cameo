For all the little things.

My love, I keep finding you in the smallest parts of my day. In the warmth of a cup held between my hands, in the song that follows me home, in the quiet moment before the city wakes, there you are.

I used to think love arrived with grand declarations. Now I think it lives in the ordinary things we choose to share. A window left open for the evening breeze. The last piece of fruit saved without a word. Your hand reaching for mine when neither of us has anything to say.

There is so much I still want to discover with you. Streets we have never walked, books we will leave unfinished, mornings when our only plan is to stay a little longer. I want the beautiful days, of course, but I want the uncertain ones too. I want to learn how to meet you gently in each of them.

If these words could become a place, I would make them a home with sunlight on the floor and room for every version of you. Until then, let this letter hold one simple promise. Wherever life takes us, I will keep choosing the small work of loving you.

With all my love.
